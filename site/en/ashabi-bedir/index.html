313 BLESSED COMPANIONS

Companions of Badr (Ashab al-Badr)

Those unmatched heroes who were personally given the glad tidings of Paradise by the Prophet ﷺ.

"O Lord! Engrave in our hearts the unwavering faith, unmatched loyalty, and blessed cause of the Companions of Badr. For their sake, do not let us stray from the straight path. Ameen."
