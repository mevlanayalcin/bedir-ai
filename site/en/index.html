How It Works?

A four-stage pipeline: from verified sources to cited answers. Quality is measured with a 313-question source-accuracy eval set.

1

Verified corpus

Verses and hadiths are vetted through classical tahqiq methodology, then versioned into the vector corpus.

2

Semantic retrieval (RAG)

Questions are embedded; only the most relevant passages retrieved from the corpus enter the model context.

3

Grounded generation

Answers are generated by Anthropic's Claude models, grounded strictly on the retrieved sources; out-of-corpus completion is locked by system prompt.

4

Citation audit

Before output, every sentence's reference is validated against the corpus; unsourced sentences never ship.
